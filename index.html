Privacy

Your photos stay yours

"We don't have servers. Your photos never touch us — they go straight from your phone to your own AI key."

Your selfie and car photo are sent to Google's Gemini API using your key, subject to Google's terms. My Other Car never receives, stores, or sees them.
Location data (EXIF/GPS) is stripped from every photo before it is uploaded.
Your API key lives only in your phone's secure storage — never in our logs, never in a crash report.
Your gallery is local. "Delete all my data" wipes the database, every image, and the key.
